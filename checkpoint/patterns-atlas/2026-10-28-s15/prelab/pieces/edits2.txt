71	<p>Now the encounter's Problem Two, formally. The question is a divisibility verdict — a team question, so class-preservation holds for exactly this question. Reduce first: \(2026 \equiv 3 \pmod{7}\). Compute in canonical coordinates: the powers of 3 cycle 3, 2, 6, 4, 5, 1 with period 6, and \(77 = 6 \cdot 12 + 5\), so \(3^{77} \equiv 3^5 \equiv 5\). 75	<p>Now classification. Compute the canonical form of g from the canonical form of f: \(f(x - t) - s = a(x - t - h)^2 + k - s\). Same a. So <strong>a is translation-invariant</strong> — the canonical form's leading coefficient is an invariant of the sliding class (P09 will make invariants a whole pattern; here one falls out of the canonical form for free).</p>
