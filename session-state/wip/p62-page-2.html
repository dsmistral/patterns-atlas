3 · The pattern, named

Pattern P62 — Sheafify on a site. Three steps, each a construction with a universality proof for its verdict. (1) Name the site. Say what the regions are and which families count as covers of what — opens of a space, patches of a cover, an abstract poset with a declared covering rule. Say what the data is: a set (or group, or ring) per region, restriction maps along inclusions, composition respected — a presheaf, with nothing forced to agree. Then ask the only diagnostic question: is there local data, honest on every overlap, that no global section witnesses? (2) Plus twice. Repair by decree: declare the consistent local reports of a region \\(U\\) — matching families on covers of \\(U\\), modulo agreement on every overlap — to be the new records \\(F^+(U)\\). One round forces locality; a second round forces gluing — on this page's finite worlds, two rounds always suffice, and the lab proves it by exhaustion; on general sites the same decree iterates further. The alternative route runs pointwise: take stalks (the germs, all local readings at a point), build the étalé space over them, and take its continuous sections. (3) Factor everything. The presheaf enters its repair by \\(\\eta\\), the class of the trivial report; and any map from \\(F\\) into a sheaf that respects restriction must factor through the repair, uniquely: \\(\\alpha = \\bar{\\alpha} \\circ \\eta\\). The repair is the least honest enlargement — and "least" is not praise but a theorem.

Or, in words: do not argue with the data, and do not mourn the world — build the world the data was asking for. Matching families are records-in-waiting; the repair is the bookkeeping that admits them; and the proof that nothing smaller or freer would do is the universal property, P31's discipline applied to whole spaces of data at once. The move's strength is what it refuses to promise: a repair that erases nothing is only possible for presheaves that already separated — \\(\\eta\\) can merge distinct records, and when it does, the merge is a verdict (the data disagreed in a way no honest world could host), measured in §5, not mourned.

The move applies when three keys line up, and each key names its own jam-mode:

Key 1 — the data must genuinely restrict. Regions, inclusions, and restriction maps that compose: \\(F(V \\cap W)\\) receives from both sides, and \\(F(W)\\) receives from \\(F(U)\\) whenever \\(W \\subseteq U\\). Jam-mode: tables of local data with no overlap bookkeeping at all — a database per island and no shared keys. Then "consistent" is not checkable, no matching family can be formed, and there is nothing to repair: the honest move is to build the overlap record first, which is data modeling, not this page's machine.

Key 2 — the site must be honest. The covers are part of the structure, and they decide everything: the same category of regions carries different sheaves under different covering rules — §4's diamond world is exactly this receipt, all \\(190\\) presheaves sheaves under the trivial site, only \\(47\\) under one extra cover. Jam-mode: too few covers, and every presheaf is a "sheaf" vacuously — the gluing law has nothing to bite, and the repair is the identity; too many covers (or covers the data cannot meet), and the repair erases records wholesale. Declare the covers, and let the lab check the census both ways.

Key 3 — the repair's price must be faced. \\(\\eta\\) embeds \\(F\\) into its repair exactly when \\(F\\) already separated; a presheaf that failed locality gets merged, and the merge is information destroyed: \\(F(X) = 2\\) records both restricting to the same island-readings become \\(1\\) record after repair — §5's Exhibit A. Jam-mode: applying sheafification as routine "cleanup" to data that was never separated — the repair silently equates distinct records, and the users of the data never learn what died. Run the census first: locality failures are a diagnosis, not a stain.

Box P62.A — the machine: the equalizer test, the plus construction, and the universality proof. The sheaf condition on a cover \\(U = \\bigcup_i U_i\\) is an equalizer diagram: the map \\(F(U) \\to \\prod_i F(U_i)\\), sending a section to its restrictions, must identify sections that agree everywhere (locality, injectivity) and hit exactly the matching families — those families \\((s_i)\\) whose members agree on every pairwise overlap, i.e. that equalize the two maps \\(\\prod_i F(U_i) \\rightrightarrows \\prod_{i,j} F(U_i \\cap U_j)\\). Two failures, two names: locality fails when the left map is not injective; gluing fails when a matching family is missed. The repair fixes both by decree. Define \\(F^+(U)\\) as the set of matching families \\((C, f)\\) — a cover \\(C\\) of \\(U\\), a reading \\(f_V \\in F(V)\\) per member, agreeing pairwise on overlaps — modulo the equivalence

\\[ (C, f) \\sim (D, g) \\quad\\text{iff}\\quad f_V|_{V \\cap W} \\;=\\; g_W|_{V \\cap W} \\quad\\text{for all } V \\in C,\\; W \\in D. \\]

Restrict a class to a smaller region by restricting each reading. The receipts, machine-checked over every presheaf of the two-island census (all \\(190\\), values of size at most \\(2\\)): one round makes every presheaf separated; a second round makes every one a sheaf; a third changes nothing; and the rounds census is honest — \\(5\\) presheaves needed no round at all (they were sheaves), \\(97\\) were repaired by one, \\(88\\) needed both. The empty stretch's law falls out of the same decree: the empty cover of \\(\\varnothing\\) has exactly one matching family (the empty one), so \\(F^+(\\varnothing)\\) is a one-point set, always — which is why \\(156\\) of the \\(190\\) fail instantly. The entry map \\(\\eta: F \\to F^{++}\\) sends a reading \\(s \\in F(U)\\) to the class of the trivial report \\((\\{U\\}, s)\\) — in two steps, one per round. And the universal property, the "least" made into a theorem: for every one of the \\(2{,}974\\) restriction-respecting maps from the \\(190\\) presheaves into the \\(5\\) sheaves, there exists exactly one map from the repair making the triangle commute — existence and uniqueness both verified by exhaustion over the whole census. Nothing about the two-step construction is arbitrary: any repair with this universal property is the same repair up to unique translation, which is P48's reading of P31.

Box P62.B — where the move lives. The construction is older than its name. Godement's 1958 reference book Topologie algébrique et théorie des faisceaux systematized the sheaf bookkeeping, including the canonical map to the étalé sheaf of germs — the "flèche" — which is this page's \\(\\eta\\) by another route. SGA 4 — Artin, Grothendieck, Verdier, seminars of 1963–64, published as Springer Lecture Notes in 1972–73 — moved the whole story to the site: regions and covers abstracted away from spaces, with sheafification defined and constructed there, and the sheaf category proved to be a topos. Giraud (1971) iterated the move one level up — gluing categories, not sets, which is the stack condition, quoted here as consistent with the literature. Lawvere–Tierney (early 1970s) re-founded the elementary topos with sheafification as an intrinsic closure operation — same quote status. The move's customers are everywhere data meets overlap: gauge physics glues field potentials on patches (the transition functions are matching-family data); databases reconcile local tables on shared keys; and every cohomology computation — P33's machine included — quietly sheafifies first, because cohomology is only defined on data that glues.
