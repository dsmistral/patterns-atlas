    Each file lost exactly 1 byte; od -c and grep -F ground-truth confirmed.
    The S43/S44 lesson re-confirmed: sed is never safe on backslash-bearing
    replacements, even "one-digit" ones. Repaired via the validated channel:
    heredoc (quoted 'EOF') + awk line-swap (getline from temp file). No sed
    anywhere in the repair.

                  delimiters; corrected to '\(e\) is not a fraction'.
