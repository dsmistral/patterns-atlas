# The Patterns Atlas — Sources & Links (consulted so far)

## The problem-solving canon (the P-index was mined and audited against these; attributions web-verified 2026-10-28)

- **Polya — *How to Solve It* (1945).** Four phases + the 67-entry heuristic dictionary. (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/How_to_Solve_It)
- **Engel — *Problem-Solving Strategies* (Springer, Problem Books in Mathematics).** 14 chapters: invariance, coloring proofs, extremal principle, box principle, enumerative combinatorics, number theory, inequalities, induction, sequences, polynomials, functional equations, geometry, games, further strategies. Verified against Goodreads / Barnes & Noble / Internet Archive listings.
- **Zeitz — *The Art and Craft of Problem Solving* (3rd ed., Wiley 2017).** Ch. 3 tactics (symmetry, extreme principle, pigeonhole, invariants), ch. 4 crossover tactics (complex numbers, generating functions), §6.3 inclusion–exclusion (publisher TOC).
