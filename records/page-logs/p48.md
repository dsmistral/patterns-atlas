**Lineage sources (§7).** Cayley's theorem (1854) embeds every group into its own translations — this page's one-object census (four equivariant functions of 256) is that theorem run as a table census. Eilenberg and Mac Lane's founding paper (1945) states the one universal property it can without the lemma's language — the identification this page dramatizes is the founding viewpoint pushed to its logical end. Nobuo Yoneda's 1954 paper on the homology of modules carries the lemma in embryonic form, the general statement circulating in Tokyo seminars around that time — the mathematics predates the name.
