  verifier itself (the page numbers held throughout): the first lgamma
  used the Lanczos product form, which overflows float64 for large z and
  read M(50) = Infinity (rewritten in log space); the first
  Crank-Nicolson gate ran dt = 0.05 (17x the explicit limit) and compared
  energy at 30 percent - the scheme energy is the square of its
  amplitude, so the honest audit is dt = 0.025 (8x the limit) landing 9.5
  percent off the exact discrete decay; the reactor RK4 first ran at
  dt = 1e-4 (1.4e-6 relative - one refinement to dt = 5e-6 closed it to
  8.6e-8); the bisection first comparison was inverted (it walked to
  zero and reported t = 0.0000000000); the RK4 wave energies first
  omitted the 1/h^2 on the potential (making the watched quantity
  kinetic-dominated), the damped-string check first paired the cross
  term with an inverted factor, and its tail rate was first measured
  over the wrong window separation (75 s read as 30 s: it reported
  -0.025 where the true rate is -0.0100, the lab -0.01001 confirmed);
  and the figure-3 vertical-line parser first counted the y-axis
  (x = 60) as a third asymptote.
