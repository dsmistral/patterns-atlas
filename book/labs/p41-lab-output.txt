RUN HISTORY (honest):
- The battery passed 51/51 on its final assembly run. Earlier draft runs
  caught defects, all in the battery's design rather than the page's math,
  all fixed before anything was licensed: the helpers were initially scoped
  inside a wrapper IIFE invisible to the pieces (moved to piece H as __H);
  __H.bottlenecks sorted by the wrong key; D02 asserted a unique optimal
  route, but the graph has two (0-2-5-8-9 and 0-2-5-8-4-7-9, both total 17),
  so the check was rewritten to demand both; D03 hardcoded the grid step
  instead of deriving it; D06 was redesigned to isolate the claim it
  licenses; E04's grid was raised to n=61 after a coarser grid blurred the
  corridor crest; Q02's factorization tolerance sat below float noise (1e-14,
  now 1e-12); Q04's expected-string join needed a "; " separator; X01 carried
  dead code (removed); X02's gates were loose (2e-3) from the draft era.
- A pre-publish adversarial audit then tightened the loose gates, and the
  page paid for one of them: E01's wall-slide claim on the rimless landscape
  said |dE/dy| = 0.32 at (0, 1.5), but the true slope there is 0.2371 (the
  draft had conflated the slope with the height, E(0, 1.5) = 0.329). The
  page now says 0.24, and E01 pins the machine value to two decimals.
  The same audit briefly "corrected" X02's crest scale from 3.5737 to a
  hand-computed 3.5735 — the tightened gate rejected the re-run, the machine
  recomputed sqrt(2*I2/pi^2) = 3.57371, and the page's 3.5737 was right all
  along; the gate now pins it at 5e-5, with the ray endpoints pinned at
  3.0913 and 1.3108 to 1e-4. The lesson is the point of the battery: hand
  arithmetic is the draft, the gates are the book.
- The three figure datasets on the page (wall, dual corridors, staircase)
  were regenerated deterministically from the same formulas as piece X
  after a hand-transcribed corridor polyline was found garbled; the
  regenerated values are the authoritative ones the page now carries.
- The census piece S pins the page at exactly 57,879 utf-8 bytes; the
  transcript below is the assembled book/labs/p41-lab.js run against the
  frozen page, twice, byte-identical both times.
