  Parameterized transform; B7 then PASS. B1–B6 passed on first run.
- Piece C: all nine checks passed on first run.

  accepted: (1) DP result array seeded one value early; (2) transfer-matrix
  builder returned after the vertical branch, never exploring horizontals
  (all zeros); (3) recurrence seed array too short (NaN at n=5). Also: the
  original D3 plan ("recurrence = DP to n=200 in floats") is IMPOSSIBLE in
  float64 — a(36) > 2^53 — so the check was split into exact agreement through
  the float64-integer range (n ≤ 35) plus mod-prime agreement (2^31−1 and
  10^9+7) to n=200. This is the page's own Exhibit C lesson applied to its lab.
- Piece E: E1 first FAILED — boundary bugs in the counting loop (π(100)
  counted 101; the last target was never recorded). Fixed; then PASS.
  E2–E6 passed on first run.
- Piece F: F3 FAILED and found a real PAGE DEFECT: two hand-rounded y-values
  in the figure's blue polyline (n=4: 224.3 → 224.4; n=7: 169.2 → 168.9),
  off the log-scale formula by 0.13 and 0.27 px. Fixed in the page; re-run PASS.
  All page numbers otherwise verified; figure defect is the only one found.
