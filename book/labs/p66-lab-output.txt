  3,901,330,906,652,795 < 2^53, and a(36) ~ 1.1082e16 > 1.2 x 2^53; D9

- Piece F1 (F1-F17): all seventeen passed. F17's FNV-1a fingerprint was
  first disclosed, then pinned. THE ADVERSARIAL VERIFIER THEN CAUGHT A REAL
  LAB DEFECT: F17's original hash multiplied in float64 past 2^53, so the
  "fingerprint" it disclosed (0xd4d2f408) was a rounding artifact, not the
  true FNV-1a. F17 was rebuilt on Math.imul (exact 32-bit multiply), the
  true fingerprint 0x046465e4 was disclosed and pinned, and the entire
  battery was re-run — the record above and below is that final run.
- Piece F2 (FG1a-FG3e): one check bug found and fixed before any verdict
  was accepted: the first number-scraper also captured the digits inside
  SVG attribute names (x1, y1), producing false FAILs in FG1b, FG2b, FG2c.
  Replaced with an explicit x1/y1/x2/y2 coordinate parser; all thirteen
  then PASS on the unchanged page.
